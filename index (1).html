Why Touchpoint BGI exists

Hi, I'm Danielle Sanson, founder of Touchpoint BGI, a Barbados-based company that works with hospitality businesses anywhere in the world.

My career is in hospitality, across hotel and restaurant operations. Over time I moved into the administrative side: payroll hours, onboarding and offboarding, and writing and refining the SOPs that teams rely on. It's where I do my best work, so it's where I've chosen to focus.

Small hospitality businesses rarely struggle because of one big mistake. More often they weaken slowly, through gaps: training that depends on who happens to be on shift, standards that live in one person's head, procedures that were never written down. Staff notice. When a pay slip is wrong, trust goes quickly, and a disgruntled team is expensive.

Wages are usually one of the largest costs in the business, yet many owners have nobody dedicated to tracking hours and making that cost easy to see and understand. No owner wants to short their staff, and none can afford to be unsure how much money is going out.

My goal is to make admin, training and payroll hours easier and more efficient for small businesses, so owners can look after their people while protecting their own stake in the company's success.
